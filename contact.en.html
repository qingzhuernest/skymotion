How can a report make a problem easier to locate?

  • Knowledge or calculation questions:Include the module, such as Sun or Moon, observation location, date and time. Describe what you saw and your question or expected result. References from textbooks or other sources are welcome.
  • Display or interaction problems:Describe the starting step, what you clicked or dragged and what happened. Include phone/computer and browser name. A screenshot or screen recording is particularly helpful.
  • Feature and teaching suggestions:Explain the concept you want to teach, intended grade level or classroom setting, and how you want students to interact and observe.

You are also welcome to share observations or classroom activities completed with this lab. Each specific piece of feedback helps this small laboratory improve.
