PLANET APPEARANCE

Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Moon and Saturn
ring maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE, based on NASA imagery/elevation data.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Download URLs, original byte hashes and adjustments: ../textures/sources.json.

Original source image bytes are retained and served locally. The publisher
enhances saturation and reconstructs some unmapped gaps. Display shaders reduce
enhancement, soften Venus cloud contrast, and approximate pale cyan ice-giant
colors with reference to the 2024 Oxford visible-color reassessment. These are
stitched maps, not live weather, dated orientations or spectrophotometric calibration.
Saturn ring lighting approximates two-sided scattering and planetary occlusion.

Earth reuses the original root earth.jpg with its existing credits. Sun is a
schematic emitter. Earth orientation is calculated separately in earth-frame.js.
Other poles and rotation phases remain illustrative. Oblateness and rings affect
display geometry only; no appearance changes physical vectors or lunar illumination.
