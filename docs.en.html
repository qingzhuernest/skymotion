Before using the models

The site combines real-date ephemerides, controlled ideal experiments and illustrative spatial models. They answer different questions. Check the time basis, observer location, ideal-model setting and scale explanation before interpreting a reading.

No account is required to observe. Saved layouts and full experiments stay in this device’s browser and do not automatically sync between devices. Model limitations, data and third-party licenses are covered in the feature and developer manuals.
