PLANET APPEARANCE

space/appearance.js generates original deterministic 1024 x 512 illustrative
surface textures locally. No external assets or runtime texture service is used.
Mercury and Moon: illustrative crater relief; Moon includes localized generic dark maria; reduced periodic relief noise.
Mars: illustrative iron-rich terrain and polar caps; not a measured Mars map.
Venus: illustrative visible-light cloud palette; no visible surface features.
Jupiter: illustrative cloud belts, turbulence and Great Red Spot appearance.
Saturn: illustrative pale belts and radially textured rings with a dark division.
Uranus / Neptune: muted blue-green atmospheric palettes with subtle cloud bands;
these are visual illustrations, not calibrated multispectral spacecraft images.
Sun: weak granular visual texture; not a solar observation or activity model.

Earth reuses ../earth.jpg and its existing root credits. Latitude/longitude in
generated textures do not identify real topography or current weather. Earth orientation is calibrated separately in earth-frame.js. Other generated
surface orientations, polar caps and Saturn ring tilt are not dated pole/rotation models.
No texture participates in ephemerides, lunar illumination or geometric sunlight
calculations. Sphere radii and positions are unchanged.

Generated maps are original project material. Rendering uses the locally bundled
Three.js under MIT; see ../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
