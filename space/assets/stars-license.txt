Changes: exclude the Sun, retain finite entries with visual magnitude <= 8.5,
retain J2000 right ascension (hours), declination (degrees), apparent visual
magnitude and B-V color index; round fields and sort by magnitude. No invented
stars or positional jitter is added. Generator: scripts/build-star-catalog.py.

Rendering: catalog J2000 directions are converted into the same fixed J2000
reference frame as the planetary display. B-V approximates blackbody color;
missing B-V uses neutral white. Magnitude-to-exposure and soft point sizes are
compressed display choices, not a calibrated observing instrument or stellar
radius measurement. Stars are a distant reference background; no dated proper
motion, annual parallax, atmospheric refraction/scintillation or local-horizon
occlusion is calculated. Do not use this background as a precision sky chart.
